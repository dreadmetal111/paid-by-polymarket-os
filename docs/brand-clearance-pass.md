# Brand Clearance Pass

## 1. Purpose

This is a preliminary brand-screening document, not legal advice.

The goal is to avoid obvious naming conflicts before renaming the public app. A deeper domain, trademark, search, and social-handle review should happen before changing the live product name.

## 2. Current Problem

The current working name "Paid by Polymarket OS" is useful internally, but it is not ideal publicly because:

- It sounds too affiliated with Polymarket.
- It is long.
- It does not clearly explain the product.
- "Paid by" can sound like a payments, job, or creator-income product.
- "OS" can sound technical or confusing for normal users.

The next public brand should feel independent, memorable, and flexible while still making the prediction-market discovery and alerts product easy to understand.

## 3. Candidate: MarkItMarket

Verdict: Reject / too risky.

Reasons:

- Too close to "MarkIt."
- There appears to be an active prediction-market-adjacent product/protocol called MarkIt.
- "MarkIt" also resembles existing financial data/market brands such as IHS Markit / S&P Global usage.
- The name could create confusion in the same general market, finance, or prediction-market space.
- Not recommended for public rename.

## 4. Candidate: Market Radar

Verdict: Reject / too crowded.

Reasons:

- Clear and descriptive, but heavily used.
- There are existing Market Radar-style products, sites, and apps.
- The phrase is generic in finance, analysis, AI market research, and analyst-report contexts.
- Possible trademark or search-confusion risk.
- Not recommended as the primary brand.

## 5. Candidate: House of Markets

Verdict: Best current candidate, pending deeper checks.

Reasons:

- More distinctive than Market Radar.
- Less directly tied to another prediction-market protocol.
- Flexible for prediction markets, sports, politics, crypto, culture, and macro events.
- Good fit for Discord/community identity.
- Can support future newsletter/alerts positioning.
- Still needs deeper checks for domain, trademark, and social handles.

## 6. Recommended Working Brand

Recommended working brand:

- House of Markets

Suggested positioning:

A prediction-market intelligence desk for discovering active markets, tracking event signals, and finding what is moving.

## 7. Suggested Taglines

- Your home base for prediction markets.
- Find the markets moving before everyone else.
- Track the events behind the odds.
- Prediction-market signals without the noise.
- Discover active markets faster.

## 8. Required Deeper Checks Before Rename

Before changing the live app, check:

- houseofmarkets.com availability
- houseofmarkets.io availability
- houseofmarkets.ai availability
- @houseofmarkets on X/Twitter
- @houseofmarkets on Discord/community naming
- GitHub/org naming if needed
- USPTO search for exact and similar names
- Google search for exact phrase
- App Store / Chrome extension / newsletter conflicts

## 9. Rename Safety Rules

- Do not claim official Polymarket partnership.
- Do not use Polymarket in the product name.
- Do not imply guaranteed profits.
- Do not promise financial advice.
- Keep "View on Polymarket" as routing language, not affiliation language.
- Keep safe preview language for Preview YES / Preview NO.
