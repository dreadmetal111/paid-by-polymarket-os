# Brand Rename Workshop

## 1. Why Rename

The current working name, "Paid by Polymarket OS," helped during early building, but it is not ideal for a broader public beta.

Reasons to rename:

- The current name sounds too officially tied to Polymarket.
- The current name is long and technical.
- "Paid by" can sound like a payments, payroll, job, or creator-income product.
- "OS" may confuse normal users who just want to understand what the website does.
- A broader name allows expansion beyond Polymarket later.
- The new name should not imply official Polymarket partnership.

The rename should keep the product's prediction-market focus while making the brand feel independent, cleaner, and easier to explain.

## 2. What The Product Is Becoming

The product is becoming a prediction-market intelligence desk for discovering, tracking, and understanding active market opportunities.

Current product strengths:

- Grouped market discovery
- Event-first market browsing
- Alert signals
- Discord alerts beta
- Outbound routing to Polymarket
- Feedback loop
- Future event detail pages

The product should feel like a focused discovery and intelligence layer, not a generic dashboard and not an official Polymarket property.

## 3. Brand Requirements

The new name should be:

- Independent from Polymarket
- Short
- Memorable
- Serious enough for finance/news users
- Flexible enough for sports, politics, crypto, culture, and macro markets
- Easy to say out loud
- Usable for a website, Discord, and future newsletter/alerts product
- Not imply guaranteed profits
- Not imply official partnership

## 4. Current Shortlist

### Forecast Desk

Pros:

- Clear, serious, and easy to say.
- Feels useful for news, politics, macro, sports, and prediction markets.
- "Desk" suggests an intelligence/productivity surface without sounding too technical.
- Works well for a newsletter, Discord, and alerts product.

Cons:

- "Forecast" is a broad word and may overlap with weather or analyst products.
- Slightly less playful than the current brand.
- Needs a strong tagline to connect it directly to prediction markets.

Best-fit positioning:

Forecast Desk is a clean intelligence desk for prediction-market signals, event discovery, and market monitoring.

### House of Markets

Pros:

- Memorable and distinctive.
- Flexible across market types and categories.
- Has personality and could support a community feel.

Cons:

- Less immediately clear as a product utility.
- "House" may sound like a media brand, community, or betting house.
- Could feel less serious for finance/news users if not designed carefully.

Best-fit positioning:

House of Markets is a more community-forward brand for people who follow prediction markets, odds, and event-driven opportunities.

### Magic Market OS

Pros:

- More playful and memorable.
- Keeps some of the original "OS" energy.
- Could work if the product becomes a broad tool suite.

Cons:

- "Magic" may feel less serious for financial/news users.
- "OS" may still confuse normal users.
- Could imply black-box predictions or certainty if not positioned carefully.

Best-fit positioning:

Magic Market OS is best suited for a more experimental toolset brand, but it may be too playful for the current public beta.

## 6. Recommended Top Five

1. Forecast Desk
2. Market Radar
3. Outcome Desk
4. House of Markets
5. The Market Wire

Why these five:

- Forecast Desk is the strongest all-around option for a serious prediction-market intelligence product.
- Market Radar is clear, active, and easy to understand.
- Outcome Desk is specific to prediction markets without depending on Polymarket.
- House of Markets has the most personality and community potential.
- The Market Wire feels timely and alert-friendly.

## 7. Suggested Taglines

- Find the markets moving before everyone else.
- Your desk for prediction-market signals.
- Discover active prediction markets faster.
- Track the events behind the odds.
- Market signals without the noise.

## 8. Rename Safety Rules

- Do not claim official Polymarket partnership.
- Do not use Polymarket in the product name.
- Do not imply guaranteed profits.
- Do not promise financial advice.
- Keep "View on Polymarket" as routing language, not affiliation language.
- Check domain, trademark, and social availability before public rename.

Then choose one working brand and update the public site copy.
