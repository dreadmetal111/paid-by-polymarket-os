House of Markets 5-Minute Prediction Market Scan

Version 1

A simple educational checklist for reading a prediction market before deciding whether to open the real market on Polymarket.

Explore live markets

Independent project. Educational use only. Not financial advice. Not an official Polymarket product or partnership.

Five-minute flow

Read the market before you react.

  1. Minute 1: Identify exactly what the question resolves.
  2. Minute 2: Read the YES probability as an implied chance, not a promise.
  3. Minute 3: Check recent movement and ask what could have changed.
  4. Minute 4: Review volume and liquidity for activity and depth.
  5. Minute 5: Compare related markets before opening the real market.
01

What does the market question actually resolve?

Read the full question slowly. Look for the event, date, exact condition, source of truth, and any wording that could change the outcome.

Example: Instead of reading only "Will a team win?", check the season, competition, and resolution date.
02

YES probability

YES probability is the market's current implied chance for the YES outcome. It can move quickly and it is not a guarantee.

Ask: Is the probability high, low, or surprising compared with what I expected?
03

Recent movement

Movement shows whether the market has shifted recently. A sharp move can mean traders are reacting to new information, changing attention, or thin liquidity.

Ask: Did something happen, or is this just noisy movement?
04

Volume

Volume helps show how much activity the market has attracted. Higher volume can make a market more worth reading, but it does not make an outcome correct.

Ask: Are people actually trading this, or is it quiet?
05

Liquidity

Liquidity is about market depth. Stronger liquidity may make prices easier to interpret, while thin markets can jump around more.

Ask: Does the market have enough depth to compare cleanly?
06

Related-market comparison

Many prediction markets belong to the same event family. Compare related outcomes before focusing on one card.

Ask: Is this market part of a bigger event with better comparisons?
