🚀 Builder Overview

Product

What is Paid by Polymarket?

Paid by Polymarket is a signal-driven prediction market terminal built to help users discover high-conviction opportunities, paper trade them, evaluate performance, and eventually route live execution through a builder-ready workflow.

User Problem

Why would traders use it?

Polymarket users often face too many markets, unclear prioritization, and little structure around decision quality. This dashboard turns noisy market discovery into a repeatable workflow: rank, analyze, simulate, track, and prepare to execute.

Polymarket Value

Why this helps the ecosystem

The product is designed to increase useful trading activity by helping users surface actionable markets faster, improve confidence before executing, and move from paper mode into live builder-routed execution as the app matures.

Monetization

How this can become a business

Paid by Polymarket can evolve into a premium signal terminal with subscription tiers, advanced alerts, portfolio analytics, execution tooling, and later a builder-routed live trading experience.
